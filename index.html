About Me

I am an Assistant Professor and academic professional with strong expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, and UI/UX design, currently associated with Parul University. I bring a balanced perspective of teaching, research, and industry-oriented skill development, with a focus on bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world applications.

My work spans AI and Generative AI education, cloud computing, deep learning, blockchain, and explainable AI, along with hands-on training in modern development ecosystems including web, mobile, and cloud platforms. I actively design project-based and experiential learning models for undergraduate and postgraduate students, ensuring practical exposure aligned with industry expectations.

In addition to teaching, I am involved in curriculum design, academic coordination, faculty development, and research guidance, and have contributed as a jury member, workshop coordinator, and program lead for various technical initiatives. I also engage in content creation and knowledge dissemination through workshops, certifications, and digital platforms, with a strong emphasis on ethical, transparent, and responsible AI systems.
